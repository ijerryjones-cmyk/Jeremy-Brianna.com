The Proposal

By Jeremy

I started off with three obstacles to overcome with this endeavor:

  • 1) I wanted this to be memorable and special on it's own, with the proposal, special in it's own way, as an added surprise to an already special occasion
  • 2) She already knew it was gonna come eventually so 'surprising' is hard to orchestrate when any big but private event is suspect
  • 3) Her mother had told me to film it, so I had to figure out how to do 'surprise' while also do 'staged' enough to catch it on camera

I had always planned to do this on a trip and Japan was my first choice so the first thing to do was to make that trip happen.

Once that was locked down I had to find the ring and a box for it. I had planned to use my mothers wedding ring, but it seems to have gone missing after her passing, but we did find one of her other rings that (from what I remember her telling me about it) has a Sapphire in it and was stored with her collection of other favored gems.

Ring in hand, I commissioned a wood and resin box on Etsy to match the ring and situational mood.

Kaiseki is a traditional high-end meal in Japan that I had decided would be the best way to do this in a controlled environment, so once it was fully mapped out where we would be on the trip timeline I reserved a room at the Ryotei Kawabun in Nagoya.

Finally, to satisfy Barb's request, I practiced getting a recording going, setting a shortcut on my watch to remotely trigger my phone to record video and acquired a case for my phone with a stand and magnet to the most diverse possibility for a decent shot.

The day-of, we took a taxi "straight" to the restaurant and I was able to surreptitiously prop my phone on my bag in the corner and line up the shot. Then for the first half of the meal I failed to realize I could dismiss the preview from my watch face without stopping the recording, so I tried to eat without showing the video on my wrist or being suspicious. Eventually the watch closed it on it's own going back to the home screen; showing that I was up to 110 BPM just prior to desert and asking the question.

All-in-all, it was expertly executed and I'm the best and I'm glad I don't have to do that again.

(She said yes BTW)
